Refined Approach: Local Flyway

Direct automation using Flyway installed on your machine

Local Automation Flow
1. Infrastructure

Terraform Apply

Provisions RDS and outputs the connection details.

▼
2. Database Schema

▼
3. Data Stream

DMS & Generator

Replication starts and Lambda begins populating the now-ready tables.
